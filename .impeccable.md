## Design Context

### Users
Teams and communities use Waddle for day-to-day conversation across desktop and mobile. They spend time in longer-running chat sessions, so the interface should stay legible and orienting over time instead of feeling intense or fatiguing.

### Brand Personality
Calm, crisp, luminous. The UI should feel clear and modern without becoming cold, and it should use accent color with restraint instead of looking flashy.

### Aesthetic Direction
Keep the existing airy "Aether" direction, but treat light and dark as equal citizens by following the system theme automatically. Avoid gamer neon, overly corporate enterprise styling, and heavy skeuomorphism.

### Design Principles
1. Let the system pick the theme with no extra friction.
2. Preserve clarity and orientation during long chat sessions.
3. Keep accents luminous but restrained so the interface stays calm.
4. Maintain strong contrast and avoid low-contrast states.
5. Respect reduced-stimulus preferences and avoid unnecessary visual intensity.
